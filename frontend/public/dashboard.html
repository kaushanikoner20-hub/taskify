Support

Adding a task

On the Dashboard, click + Add Task above your task cards. Set a due date to have "days left" calculated automatically.

Logging hours

On the Dashboard's Tasks Progress card, click + Log Hours. Hours are added to today's real weekday bar in the chart.

Tracking progress

Use the Track section in the sidebar to update any task's progress percentage directly.

Adding team members

On the Dashboard's Batchmates card, click + Add and enter their name and role.

Updating your profile

Go to Settings in the sidebar to change your name, role, or password.
